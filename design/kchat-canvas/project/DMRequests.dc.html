Message requests and Who can message me
Like Instagram: a first message from someone you don't follow waits in Requests, quietly. You read it without them knowing, then Accept, Delete or Block. The sender gets one message until you accept. Settings decides who can reach you at all. Motion is calm: rows fade up 8px, the request panel crossfades into the composer on Accept, nothing bounces.
