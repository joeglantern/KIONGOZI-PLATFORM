Polls in posts: ask, tap, see where Kenya stands
Like X. The post text is the question, 2 to 4 choices of up to 25 characters, open for 1 hour to 7 days (1 day by default). A poll post has no photos or video. One tap votes, instantly; the bars then fill left to right on a calm ease-out (420ms, no bounce) with your choice ticked. Votes are final. The author sees results straight away. Ended polls say Final results and the winner is bold.
1 · Composer
Poll button sits with the media tools. While a poll is on, photo and video tools step back (and the poll button greys out once media is added). Post stays off until the question and two choices are in.
