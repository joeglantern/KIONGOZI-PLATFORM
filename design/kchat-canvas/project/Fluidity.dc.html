Feel and fluidity
Things people feel without noticing. A calm "You're all caught up" line in Following, tab re-taps that scroll to the top then refresh on every main list, Reduce Motion honoured everywhere (fades instead of movement, loops stand still), an honest "Edited" label with the history one tap away, and one haptic vocabulary. Nothing bounces: the caught-up row fades in at 220ms, the history sheet glides up on the app's ease-out.

5 · Reduce Motion and one haptic language
With Reduce Motion on (the phone's own setting): entrances fade without rising, sheets fade in place, ambient loops (Baraza, waving hand, stick, shimmer, paper boil) stand still on a good frame, confetti is skipped. Off, nothing changes.
Entrancefade with 12px rise, becomes fade only
Sheetglides up, becomes fade in place
Ambient loopsrun, become a still frame
Confettiplays, is skipped
Tapselection tick: tabs, chips, open, share, pin
Likelight impact: like, repost, follow, send, vote
Successsuccess: posted, edit saved, request accepted
Destructivewarning: delete, block, remove
Explore: every tappable has a spoken label (trends, people, show more, cancel, clear all, recent searches, results).
