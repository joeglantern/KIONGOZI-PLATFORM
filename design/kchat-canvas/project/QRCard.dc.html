Profile QR code, cut from paper
The code is printed on paper: a torn cream patch (Paper) or a paper card (Brush), on a coloured sheet with grain, two cut-paper shapes and one brush mark. Your photo sits in the middle (error correction H). Tap the card or a swatch to change the paper: six themes change the background, the code's ink, the brush colour and the shapes together. The app draws the card live; Share and Save send the same card rendered on the server at 1080×1350. Every export below decodes with jsQR and ZXing.
