Trust and security: where you're logged in, new sign-in alerts, muted words
What Instagram, WhatsApp and Google do so people feel safe: see every phone on your account and log one out, get told when a new phone signs in, and quietly hide words you're tired of. Plain grouped settings, one line of state under each row, no alarms. Motion is calm: rows fade up 12px, a logged-out row fades away in 240ms ease-out, nothing bounces.
